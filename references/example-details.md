## Cross-cutting rules learned from the corpus

- **Gray has three distinct meanings — never merge them.** *Default/selected baseline* (`representation-ablation`, `others-example1`); *best/second-best standing* (`main_table-example1`: deeper gray = best, light gray = second-best, stated in its caption); *population membership* (`guidance-comparison`: gray marks the class-conditional population, not rank).
- **Caption weight and position are reference-specific.** Bold lead, bold first sentence, fully regular serif, and centered sans-serif captions all occur. Do not impose one universal bold-lead-only rule. Captions may sit below the tabular block (`diffusion-main`) or span panels (`representation-ablation`).
- **Limited vertical rules are allowed where the source shows them** at semantic block boundaries; booktabs-first, no full grid. Source-specific extent is recorded per case.
- **Preserve source-provided deltas and ranks**; never silently recompute a reported source delta from rounded means. New synthetic or user-authorized calculations are allowed when the baseline and units are explicit. Gains in percentage points are labeled as such. Parameter strings such as `30B-A3B`, `675+49M`, `700 M`, `2B` are identity strings, not scalars.

## How to reuse these lessons

Retrieve by task, column count and distinguishing features (rotated heads, coverage symbols, paired gains, gray default rows, population bands). Read the matching case and first record source crop geometry, caption/table and body/caption ratios, wrap, font weight/slant, rules, gutters and cell emphasis. Copy only observed, relevant decisions onto the user's own data. The structured generator covers **one header-group level**; deeper headers, multirow, bespoke macro grammars, pseudocode and any original-reference reproduction are agent-authored with a keyed ledger. Typography is source-conditioned, not a universal booktabs/bold-italic preset. Source-preserving reconstruction, compilation, visual correspondence and publication-size readability are distinct checks — none of them is pixel identity, exact font identity, venue compliance or independent verification of results.
